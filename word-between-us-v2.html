How to play

  1. Each player draws 4 vowels and 7 consonants, then secretly makes a valid word using any number of those letters.
  2. After both players lock in, your 11 scrambled letters are effectively swapped: you see the rack that could make your opponent's word.
  3. Take turns either activating one clue or making one guess.
  4. When you activate a clue, your opponent receives that clue's berry value. If you guess wrong, your opponent receives 2 berries.
  5. If you correctly guess while you have more berries than your opponent, you win immediately. If you're tied or behind, your opponent keeps taking turns; their clues and misses can give you enough berries to move ahead.
  6. If both secret words are solved and the berry totals are tied, use the 4-letter speed tiebreak.
The clue names and wording here are original, while the scoring/turn structure intentionally follows the style of the physical game you asked to emulate.
