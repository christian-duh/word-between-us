How to play

  1. Each player gets a 9-letter rack and makes a secret 4–9 letter word using only those letters.
  2. Once both words are locked, each player starts with 12 points.
  3. Spend points to ask clue questions. Clue answers are calculated automatically from the secret word.
  4. You can guess at any time. A wrong guess costs 1 point; a correct guess solves the word.
  5. The round ends once both players solve. The player with more points left wins; equal scores tie.

This is an original word game inspired by the general idea of two-player secret-word deduction games; it does not reproduce another game’s cards, art, or rules text.
